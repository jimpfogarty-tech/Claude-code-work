How to play

        

            
A secret code of coloured pegs is hidden at the top of the board. Your job
               is to work out exactly which colours it uses, and in exactly what order,
               before you run out of rows.

            
Place pegs into the active row and press Check. The small
               key pegs on the right tell you how close you got:

            
    
                
  • 
                    Solid — one peg is the right colour in the right place.
    • 
                
  • 
                    Hollow — one peg is the right colour in the wrong place.
    • 
                
  • 
                    Empty — that's one peg with no match at all.
    • 
            

            
Key pegs never tell you which of your pegs they refer to —
               that's the puzzle. Colours may repeat in the code.

            
Controls. Tap a colour to drop it into
               the next open slot, or drag it where you want. Tap a placed peg to lift it
               out. On a keyboard: number keys place a colour, Backspace removes the last
               one, Enter checks the row.
